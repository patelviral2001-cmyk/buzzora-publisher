Buzzora — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Buzzora (Instagram @buzz.ora72, Facebook page Buzzora72, YouTube @Buzzora72) is a pop-culture media page. The "Buzzora72" Google app is an internal publishing tool used only by the Buzzora team to upload Buzzora's own videos to Buzzora's own YouTube channel.

What data the tool uses

The tool requests one Google permission, youtube.upload, for the Buzzora channel only. It stores a single OAuth refresh token as an encrypted secret, used solely to upload videos we create. It does not read, collect, sell or share any other person's data.

Third parties

Videos and captions are sent only to YouTube, Instagram and Facebook through their official APIs. Use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
